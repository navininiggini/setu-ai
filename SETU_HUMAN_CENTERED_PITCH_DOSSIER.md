## 1. The Human Reality Behind the Numbers

Every year, the Parliament of India allocates over **₹5,000 Crores** under the Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS). Across 543 constituencies, this money is intended to be the lifeblood of grassroots democracy: community halls, rural high-mast solar lights, crematoriums, maternity wings, and drinking water pipelines.

Yet, every two years, when the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) publishes its audit reports, the same tragic patterns emerge:
* **The Ghost Projects**: Millions of rupees disbursed for pavement roads and borewells that never broke ground.
* **The "Smurfing" Trick**: A ₹25 Lakh community project is intentionally sliced into five separate contracts of ₹4,92,000 each. Why? Because statutory rules (MoSPI Rule 14.2) require open public e-tenders for any project exceeding ₹5.00 Lakhs. By keeping each work order at ₹4.92 Lakhs, corrupt networks award the contracts to their cousins and shell entities without a single honest competitor ever knowing.
* **The Two-Year Autopsy**: By the time government inspectors discover these paper anomalies, 18 to 24 months have passed. The contractor has dissolved the company, the funds have vanished, and the community is left with empty promises.

**SETU** was born to change this reality—not by adding more bureaucracy, but by placing an instantaneous, mathematical guardian right at the point where decisions are made.

### **Page 2: Proposed Solution — Stopping Leakage at the Gate**
* **The Big Shift**: Traditional audit is an autopsy; SETU is an emergency room triage. We move the moment of audit intervention from two years *after* money has been stolen to the exact moment *before* the District Magistrate signs the approval.
* **Point 1 — Continuous Pre-Sanction Gatekeeping**:
  Automatically ingests raw project proposals from MoSPI’s e-SAKSHI portal, contractor bids, estimated vs tendered costs, and GPS coordinates in real-time, executing instant statutory triage (<200 ms) before treasury cash is released.
* **Point 2 — 8-Model Orthogonal Forensic AI Triangulation**:
  Deconstructs raw project proposals across 7 specialized domain anomaly checks (cost outliers, spatial density, single-bid cartels, contractor overload, milestone velocity, and entity network graphs) fused with a Calibrated Supervised XGBoost Classifier to eliminate false alarms.
* **Point 3 — Uncovering Covert ₹5L Tender Splitting**:
  Automatically catches corrupt practices where large civil projects are deliberately sliced into multiple smaller works just below the statutory ₹5 Lakh e-tender ceiling (e.g., ₹4,92,000) to bypass mandatory competitive bidding under MoSPI Rule 14.2.
* **Point 4 — Evidence-Based Prioritization & Legal Reason Traces**:
  Assigns every proposal an instant clearance tier (`AUTOMATIC_CLEARANCE`, `CONDITIONAL_APPROVAL`, `MANDATORY_TECHNICAL_AUDIT`, or `REJECT_AND_INVESTIGATE`) with plain-language Tree SHAP legal explanations citing General Financial Rules (GFR 155), removing guesswork for sanctioning officers.
