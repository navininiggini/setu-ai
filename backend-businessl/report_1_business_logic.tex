\section{Topic 1 --- The Real-World Problem}

\subsection{Business Meaning}

India's \textbf{Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS)} allocates \INR{}5 crore per year to every Member of Parliament to fund local infrastructure --- handpumps, anganwadis, rural roads, community halls, school buildings, sanitation works. Each MP recommends works to District Magistrates (DMs) who sanction, tender, and disburse funds to Implementing District Agencies (IDAs) and contractors.

\textbf{The monitoring crisis:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Thousands of concurrent active works across hundreds of districts
\item Hundreds of MPs, thousands of contractors, 1,238 unique IDAs
\item Manual audit by District Magistrates is impossible at this scale
\item By the time a ghost project or overpricing fraud is noticed, funds are already released and irrecoverable
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Frauds that occur in practice (each is a distinct anomaly typology):}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Fraud Type} & \textbf{What Happens} \\
\midrule
\texttt{GHOST\_PROJECT} & Money fully disbursed; no physical work ever done \\
\texttt{COST\_OVERRUN} & Project bill-of-quantities inflated 3× peer median; contractor pockets difference \\
\texttt{BID\_RIGGING} & Contractor cartel submits identical/near-identical bids to ensure predetermined winner \\
\texttt{PAYMENT\_STRUCTURING} & Large \INR{}15L+ work split into sub-\INR{}5L fragments to bypass GFR Rule 161 open-tender mandate \\
\texttt{VENDOR\_CAPTURE} & One MP routes 70\%+ of constituency MPLADS funds to a single politically-connected IDA \\
\texttt{ABANDONED\_WORK} & Financial disbursement is 80\% complete but physical ground progress is near zero \\
\texttt{ROUND\_TRIPPING\_VENDOR} & Same contractor wins consecutive works across multiple MPs in a coordinated arrangement \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}


\textbf{The decision that must be made, weekly:}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=blue!4!white,colframe=blue!60!black,arc=1.5mm,boxrule=0.7pt,left=8pt,right=8pt,top=6pt,bottom=6pt]
\textit{"Of the 5,000+ currently active MPLADS works, which should the District Magistrate's field inspection team physically visit THIS week?"}\\
\end{tcolorbox}

\textbf{Investigator bandwidth constraint:} A DM's office can realistically field-inspect ~20–30 projects per month. Without intelligent prioritization, they inspect randomly --- not by statistical risk signal.

\textbf{Information available:} Sanction records, disbursement installment transactions, physical progress reports, GPS geotags, Measurement Book (MB) entries, contractor registration records, Utilization Certificates (UCs), tender documentation.

\textbf{Information NOT available at inference time:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Ground-truth fraud labels (only present for the synthetic evaluation benchmark)
\item Real-time GPS verification of physical assets
\item Inter-state contractor ownership linkages
\end{itemize}

\section{Topic 2 --- What the System Actually Does}

\subsection{One-Sentence Summary}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=blue!4!white,colframe=blue!60!black,arc=1.5mm,boxrule=0.7pt,left=8pt,right=8pt,top=6pt,bottom=6pt]
\textbf{"SETU takes every MPLADS project, runs it through 7 independent anomaly detectors and 1 supervised model, triangulates all signals into a risk score (0–100), tells investigators WHY a project is suspicious in plain English, and assigns a triage priority (IMMEDIATE / PRIORITY / ROUTINE)."}\\
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Complete Business Logic --- Step by Step}

\textbf{Step 1 --- Data Ingestion \& Validation}
12 relational CSV datasets (5,000 projects, 13,866 payment records, etc.) are loaded. Every FK relationship (16 total) is checked for integrity. Zero orphan records tolerated.

\textbf{Step 2 --- Aggregation (The Many-to-One Problem)}
Raw payment installment rows (13,866 $\rightarrow$ 29 features per project) and progress inspection records are collapsed into one row per project. ML models require exactly one feature vector per project.

\textbf{Step 3 --- Feature Join}
All 12 tables are left-joined on \texttt{project\_id} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{project\_master\_features.csv}: 5,000 rows × 239 columns. Identity strings stripped. Labels permanently quarantined.

\textbf{Step 4 --- Feature Audit \& Registry}
239 columns classified into NUMERICAL, CATEGORICAL, TEMPORAL, IDENTIFIER, EXCLUDE. A controlled feature registry tells each model which columns it may legally use.

\textbf{Step 5 --- 7 Independent Domain Models (Isolation Forest per domain)}

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l l l Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Domain} & \textbf{Algorithm} & \textbf{Primary Signal} \\
\midrule
M1 & Financial & Isolation Forest + Peer MAD & Cost overpricing, disbursement velocity \\
M2 & Geospatial & Spatial DBSCAN / LOF & Geographic clustering anomalies \\
M3 & Procurement & Isolation Forest & Tender rigging, single-bid anomalies \\
M4 & Contractor/Agency & Entity aggregation & Vendor capture, capacity strain \\
M5 & Payment & Sequential anomaly & Smurfing, structuring near statutory caps \\
M6 & Progress & Trajectory slope & Ghost projects, physical-financial gap \\
M7 & Graph & PageRank + Degree Centrality & MP-IDA collusive networks \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}


Models are \textbf{not allowed to see each other's outputs during training} --- this guarantees independent evidence signals.

\textbf{Step 6 --- Supervised Calibrated Predictor (Model 8, XGBoost)}
Takes all 7 domain scores + context $\rightarrow$ \texttt{fraud\_probability} (0.0–1.0) + \texttt{predicted\_typology}. SHAP attributions explain each prediction.

\textbf{Step 7 --- Risk Fusion Engine}
Triangulates all 8 signals $\rightarrow$ \texttt{overall\_risk\_score} (0–100), \texttt{risk\_level}, \texttt{investigation\_priority}, \texttt{synthesized\_reasons[1..5]}.

\textbf{Step 8 --- RAG Hard Negative Resolution}
Cross-references flagged works against statutory knowledge: flood moratoriums, Election Commission MCC blackouts, Schedule of Rates benchmarks, statutory public agency designations. Scores calibrated downward for legitimate outliers.

\textbf{Step 9 --- API Serving + Frontend Dashboard}
FastAPI exposes 12 REST endpoints. Next.js dashboard provides ranked risk queue, 7-domain radar charts, network graph, full SHAP evidence dossiers.

\subsection{Mathematical Definitions}

\textbf{Project Feature Vector:}

\[ P_i = [p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_{239}] \in \mathbb{R}^{239} \]

After feature audit: 142 numerical, 19 categorical (one-hot), 15 temporal (elapsed-day intervals), 56 EXCLUDED.

\textbf{Domain Anomaly Score:}
\[ s_k(P_i) \in [0.0, 100.0], \quad k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\} \]

\textbf{Supervised Fraud Probability:}
\[ \hat{p}_\text{fraud}(P_i) \in [0.0, 1.0] \]

\textbf{Overall SETU Risk Score:}
\[ R(P_i) \in [0.0, 100.0] \]

\textbf{Risk Tier:}
\[ T(P_i) \in \{\text{CRITICAL}, \text{HIGH}, \text{MEDIUM}, \text{LOW}\} \]

\textbf{Investigation Priority:}
\[ \Pi(P_i) \in \{\text{IMMEDIATE}, \text{PRIORITY}, \text{ROUTINE}\} \]

\textbf{Reason Trace Vector:}
\[ \mathbf{r}(P_i) = [r_1, r_2, r_3, r_4, r_5] \]

Each $r_j$ is a ranked human-readable audit string.

\section{Topic 4 --- Business Objective $\rightarrow$ Mathematical Objective}

\subsection{Why Accuracy Is the Wrong Metric}

Dataset has ~20\% fraud prevalence. A naïve "all normal" model achieves 80\% accuracy but is completely useless operationally. SETU instead optimizes \textbf{fraud concentration in the investigation queue}.

\subsection{Formal Mathematical Objective}

\[ \text{Maximize} \quad \text{Enrichment@}k = \frac{\text{FraudRate}(\text{Top-}k\% \text{ by } R(P_i))}{\text{BaseFraudRate}_\text{population}} \]

Subject to:
\[ \text{FalsePositiveRate}(\text{is\_hard\_negative}, \text{Top-}k\%) \leq \varepsilon \]

\subsection{The Risk Fusion Formula (from \texttt{risk\_fusion\_engine.py} L78)}

\[ R(P_i) = 0.40 \cdot (\hat{p}_\text{fraud} \times 100) + 0.35 \cdot \bar{s}_\text{weighted} + 0.25 \cdot \max_{k=1}^{7}(s_k) \]

Where:
\[ \bar{s}_\text{weighted} = \frac{\sum_{k=1}^{7} w_k \cdot s_k}{\sum_{k=1}^{7} w_k} \]

\section{Key Takeaways --- Report 1}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{SETU is a triage tool.} It ranks 5,000 projects by investigation priority. It does not prosecute.
\item \textbf{Multi-evidence triangulation} is the core principle. A single domain flag without supervised corroboration only reaches an awareness floor (≥38), not CRITICAL.
\item \textbf{Hard negatives are a first-class design concern.} A remote single-bid handpump in a flood-prone district is statistically suspicious but operationally legitimate. SETU explicitly handles this.
\item \textbf{The evaluation metric is enrichment, not accuracy.} Goal: when an investigator examines the top 5\% of flagged projects, >68\% should be genuine fraud scenarios.
\item \textbf{System is at Stage 2B.} Financial Model is fully validated (31/31 tests passing). Models 2–8 have templates but await implementation.
\end{enumerate}
