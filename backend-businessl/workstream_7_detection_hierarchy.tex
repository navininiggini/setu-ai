\section{Executive Overview: The Multi-Layer Detection Hierarchy}

Following the construction of the feature engineering store and AI/ML risk fusion models in Workstreams 5 and 6, \textbf{Workstream 7} engineered the core anomaly detection hierarchy of \textbf{SATARK v2} (Layers L0 through L4) and synthesized them through a unified \textbf{Cascading Detection Pipeline Orchestrator}:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Task 7.1 (Layer 0 --- Point Anomaly Sentinel):} Engineered an ultra-fast, zero-overhead deterministic statutory rule engine executing \textbf{19 statutory verification checks} covering date chronology, negative values, \INR{}5 Crore annual ceiling, geographic bounds, document completeness (MB, UC, AS, Geotag), single-bid tenders, compressed tender windows, and rapidfuzz NLP matching on the MPLADS Annexure-II Negative List. Achieved median latency of \textbf{0.1508 ms} (SLA: $< 4.0$ ms) with \textbf{24/24 unit tests passing}.
\item \textbf{Task 7.2 (Layer 1 --- Contextual Anomaly Engine):} Implemented dynamic peer-group benchmarking using a \textbf{4D KDTree} (Latitude, Longitude, Terrain Ruggedness Index, and District Capacity). Built terrain-aware duration, burn-rate, and cost-per-progress deviation scoring. Successfully eliminated false delay alerts on difficult terrain projects (e.g. Leh, Ladakh hill works pass with duration $z \le 1.0$ vs. $z > 6.0$ in plain baseline), with \textbf{7/7 unit tests passing}.
\item \textbf{Task 7.3 (Layer 2 --- Collective Anomaly \& Portfolio Engine):} Built a multi-project collective anomaly analyzer detecting:
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Fund-splitting ("Smurfing"):} Multiple works recommended by the same MP to the same contractor within 14 days just below the \INR{}10 Lakh mandatory e-tendering threshold ($p < 10^{-6}$).
\item \textit{Contractor Overload:} Contractors exceeding 10 active works past declared execution capacity.
\item \textit{Sector Skew:} Single sectors consuming $> 70\%$ of allocations to the exclusion of SC/ST mandatory quotas.
\item \textit{March Rush Churn:} $5\times$ year-end surge spikes. Verified with \textbf{8/8 unit tests passing}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Task 7.4 (Layer 3 --- Temporal Anomaly \& Electoral Overlay):} Implemented \textbf{Bayesian Online Change-Point Detection (BOCPD)} using Adams \& MacKay recursive run-length message passing in log-space with Student's $t$ predictive distributions, identifying structural shifts in district spending tempo. Coupled with a \textbf{90-Day Pre-Election Sanction Rush Detector} anchored against the 16th, 17th, and 18th Lok Sabha election dates, alerting on pre-election sanction spikes with zero follow-through progress 6 months post-election while remaining immune to natural post-monsoon construction surges. Verified with \textbf{9/9 unit tests passing}.
\item \textbf{Task 7.5 (Layer 4 --- Relational Graph \& Network Anomaly Engine):} Integrated an asynchronous graph traversal engine querying \textbf{Neo4j AuraDB}:
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Closed 3-contractor revolving bid cartel rings sharing registered premises with cross-collusion edges (risk score: \textbf{0.95}, SLA: $\ge 0.85$).
\item Monopolistic vendor concentration ($> 60\%$ of civil works under a single District Authority).
\item Shell vendor detection (contractors registered within 30 days of high-value awards).
\item Conflict-of-interest closed triads among MPs, private trusts/societies, and commercial contractors. Verified with \textbf{9/9 unit tests passing}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Task 7.6 (Cascading Detection Pipeline Orchestrator):} Implemented the unified \texttt{DetectionPipeline} coordinating real-time ($< 100$ ms) triage and comprehensive batch sweeps. Enforces the unbreakable core invariant: \textbf{"Higher layers add evidence; lower-layer deterministic flags are NEVER silenced by higher layers"}. Benchmarked median real-time execution latency at \textbf{32.25 ms} (SLA: $< 100$ ms), bringing the total test suite to \textbf{238 passed tests with zero failures}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Detailed Task-by-Task Implementation Analysis}

\subsection{Task 7.1: Layer 0 --- Point Anomaly Sentinel (\texttt{backend/detection/layer0\_sentinel.py})}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Objective:} Establish the fastest, lowest false-positive statutory defense line in public finance analytics, validating works against 19 non-negotiable statutory rules.
\item \textbf{Rules Implemented Across 7 Categories:}
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Date Sequence Integrity (Rules 1–4):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_date\_chronology}: Enforces $\text{Sanction Date} \le \text{Start Date} \le \text{Completion Date}$.
\item \texttt{check\_expenditure\_before\_sanction}: Disbursement date prior to administrative sanction date is strictly impossible (GFR 2017 Rule 140).
\item \texttt{check\_completion\_before\_start}: Completion date recorded before work commencement (MPLADS Guidelines §4.2).
\item \texttt{check\_future\_dates}: Milestone dates occurring after system run timestamp.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Financial Bounds (Rules 5–8):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_negative\_amounts}: Any financial value $< 0.0$ (GFR 2017 Rule 86).
\item \texttt{check\_expenditure\_exceeds\_sanction}: Total expenditure $> (\text{Sanction Amount} + \text{Rs.\,}100 \text{ margin})$.
\item \texttt{check\_sanction\_ceiling}: Individual work sanction exceeding the statutory \INR{}5 Crore limit ($\text{Rs.\,}50,000,000$).
\item \texttt{check\_cost\_estimate\_deviation}: Estimated cost deviating by $> 25\%$ from sanctioned amount.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Geographic Validation (Rules 9–10):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_coordinate\_bounds\_india}: Latitude outside $8.0^\circ - 37.0^\circ\text{N}$ or Longitude outside $68.0^\circ - 97.5^\circ\text{E}$.
\item \texttt{check\_constituency\_boundary}: Bounding box checks against district territory.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Document Completeness (Rules 11–14):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_missing\_sanction\_order}: Administrative Sanction (AS) order missing.
\item \texttt{check\_missing\_measurement\_book}: Measurement Book (MB) verification absent when fund disbursement $> \text{Rs.\,}0$.
\item \texttt{check\_missing\_utilization\_cert}: Utilization Certificate (UC) absent on completed projects.
\item \texttt{check\_missing\_geotagged\_photos}: Project marked physically completed without geotagged before/after photographic proof.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Status Consistency (Rules 15–16):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_complete\_no\_expenditure}: Status "Completed" but expenditure equals zero.
\item \texttt{check\_expenditure\_no\_progress}: Financial expenditure $> 50\%$ with physical progress recorded as zero.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Procurement Integrity (Rules 17–18):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_single\_bid\_tender}: Single-bid tender awarded without retendering or justification (GFR Rule 173).
\item \texttt{check\_bid\_window\_compressed}: Tender notice duration compressed below statutory 7-day minimum window.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Compliance NLP (Rule 19):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \texttt{check\_negative\_list\_nlp}: Scans project title and description using Rapidfuzz fuzzy ratio ($\ge 80\%$) against MPLADS Annexure-II Prohibited Categories (religious shrines, statues, memorials, private assets, staff luxury cars).
\item \textbf{Latency \& Benchmark:} Evaluates all 19 rules in \textbf{0.1508 ms} median execution time, well within the 4.0 ms SLA. 24/24 unit tests passing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Task 7.2: Layer 1 --- Contextual Anomaly Engine (\texttt{backend/detection/layer1\_contextual.py})}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Objective:} Eliminate catastrophic false-positive alerts caused by rigid nation-wide thresholds through dynamic peer-group benchmarking and terrain awareness.
\item \textbf{Key Engineering Mechanism:}
\item \textit{4D KDTree Clustering:} Embeds works in a 4-dimensional normalized coordinate space:
\end{itemize}
\[ \mathbf{x} = \left[ \frac{\text{lat} - \mu_{\text{lat}}}{\sigma_{\text{lat}}}, \frac{\text{lon} - \mu_{\text{lon}}}{\sigma_{\text{lon}}}, \frac{\text{TRI} - \mu_{\text{TRI}}}{\sigma_{\text{TRI}}}, \frac{\text{Cap} - \mu_{\text{Cap}}}{\sigma_{\text{Cap}}} \right] \]
where $\text{TRI}$ is the Terrain Ruggedness Index from SRTM 90m digital elevation models, and $\text{Cap}$ is the District Administrative Capacity Index.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Dynamic Z-Score Benchmarking:} Evaluates works strictly against the nearest $k=50$ geographic-terrain-administrative peers:
\end{itemize}
\[ z_{\text{duration}} = \frac{\text{duration}_{\text{observed}} - \mu_{\text{peer}}}{\sigma_{\text{peer}}} \]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Thresholds:}
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Duration Z-Score $> 2.5 \implies$ \texttt{CONTEXTUAL\_DELAY\_EXCESSIVE}.
\item Expenditure Burn Rate $< 0.20 \times \text{peer median} \implies$ \texttt{CONTEXTUAL\_STALLED\_BURN\_RATE}.
\item Cost per Physical Unit $> 1.80 \times \text{peer median} \implies$ \texttt{CONTEXTUAL\_COST\_INFLATION}.
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Validation Outcome:} In high-altitude terrain (Leh, Ladakh; TRI $= 7.82$), a bridge project taking 480 days had a raw plain-terrain z-score of $+6.2$. When evaluated through Layer 1's 4D KDTree against fellow Himalayan works, its contextual duration z-score was $0.85 \le 1.0$, correctly passing without false alarm. 7/7 unit tests passing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Task 7.3: Layer 2 --- Collective Anomaly \& Portfolio Engine (\texttt{backend/detection/layer2\_collective.py})}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Objective:} Identify multi-project compound collusion patterns that appear completely benign when projects are inspected in isolation.
\item \textbf{Pattern Detectors:}
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Fund-Splitting ("Smurfing"):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Identifies clusters of works sanctioned by the same MP and awarded to the same vendor, each priced just below the \INR{}10 Lakh mandatory e-procurement threshold (e.g. \INR{}9.80 Lakh, \INR{}9.75 Lakh) occurring within a 14-day window.
\item Formulated via combinatorial binomial testing: the probability of 4 independent works from a \INR{}5 Crore portfolio landing simultaneously in the $[\text{Rs.\,}9.5\text{L}, \text{Rs.\,}9.99\text{L}]$ corridor within 14 days by chance is $p < 10^{-6}$.
\item Flag: \texttt{COLLECTIVE\_SMURFING\_SUSPECTED}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Contractor Overload:}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Detects contractors winning $> 10$ active simultaneous civil projects exceeding their audited staff, equipment, and equipment capacity index.
\item Flag: \texttt{COLLECTIVE\_CONTRACTOR\_OVERLOAD}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Sector Skew \& Quota Exclusion:}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Detects districts where a single sector (e.g., community halls or roads) consumes $> 70\%$ of the annual fund, breaching statutory mandatory allocations (15\% for Scheduled Castes and 7.5\% for Scheduled Tribes).
\item Flag: \texttt{COLLECTIVE\_SECTOR\_SKEW}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{March Rush Churn Anomaly:}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Flags year-end spending sprees where sanctions in the final month of the fiscal year surge $> 5\times$ above the 11-month moving average without technical sanctions in place.
\item Flag: \texttt{COLLECTIVE\_CHURN\_ANOMALY}.
\item \textbf{Validation Outcome:} Injected 4-work smurfing pattern (four works at \INR{}9.80 Lakh awarded to Contractor \texttt{CON-042} within 10 days) successfully flagged as \texttt{COLLECTIVE\_SMURFING\_SUSPECTED} with category \texttt{POTENTIAL\_FRAUD\_CONCERN}. 8/8 unit tests passing.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1em}\hrule\vspace{1em}

\subsection{Task 7.4: Layer 3 --- Temporal Anomaly \& Electoral Overlay (\texttt{backend/detection/layer3\_temporal.py})}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Objective:} Identify structural disruptions in spending tempo and politically motivated election-eve sanction surges.
\item \textbf{Mathematical Foundations:}
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Bayesian Online Change-Point Detection (BOCPD):}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Implements Adams \& MacKay (2007) changepoint recursion over monthly district expenditure series $x_{1:t}$.
\item Maintains run-length posterior $P(r_t | x_{1:t})$ using an exact conjugate Student's $t$ predictive distribution over unknown mean $\mu$ and precision $\tau$.
\item Operates strictly in \textbf{log-space} using \texttt{scipy.special.logsumexp} to eliminate floating-point underflow:
\end{itemize}
\[ \ln P(r_t=0, x_{1:t}) = \ln \left( \sum_{r_{t-1}} P(r_{t-1}, x_{1:t-1}) \cdot \pi_{\text{hazard}} \cdot P(x_t | r_{t-1}) \right) \]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Constant hazard rate $H = 1/\lambda$ where $\lambda = 36$ months. Abrupt shifts ($P(r_t = 0) > 0.40$) trigger \texttt{TEMPORAL\_STRUCTURAL\_SHIFT}.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textit{Election-Eve Sanction Rush Detector:}
\end{enumerate}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item Anchors on the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) enforcement window (90 days prior to election date) across the 16th (May 2014), 17th (May 2019), and 18th (June 2024) Lok Sabha elections.
\item Flags disproportionate pre-election recommendation spikes ($> 2.5\times$ baseline volume) where physical progress remains $< 10\%$ six months post-election.
\item \textit{Seasonal Immunity:} Normal post-monsoon construction surges (October–November) exhibit steady follow-through progress ($> 30\%$ at 6 months) and are rigorously shielded from alerting.
\item \textbf{Validation Outcome:} Test cases accurately detected pre-election sanction spikes for 16th and 17th Lok Sabha elections while passing post-monsoon October surge baselines. 9/9 unit tests passing.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1em}\hrule\vspace{1em}

\subsection{Task 7.5: Layer 4 --- Relational Graph \& Network Anomaly Engine (\texttt{backend/detection/layer4\_network.py})}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt, topsep=3pt]
\item \textbf{Objective:} Traverse entity relationships across MPs, District Authorities, Implementing Agencies, Contractors, and Works in Neo4j AuraDB to uncover collusion networks.

\section{Mathematical Formulations \& Verification}

\subsection{1. Bayesian Online Change-Point Detection (BOCPD)}
Let $x_t$ denote the monthly expenditure in district $d$ at month $t$. The run-length $r_t$ represents the time elapsed since the most recent changepoint. Given observations $x_{1:t}$, the posterior over $r_t$ is computed recursively:
\[ P(r_t, x_{1:t}) = \sum_{r_{t-1}} P(r_t | r_{t-1}) \cdot P(x_t | r_{t-1}, x_{t}^{(r)}) \cdot P(r_{t-1}, x_{1:t-1}) \]
where the transition probability (hazard function) is:
\[ P(r_t | r_{t-1}) = \begin{cases} H(r_{t-1} + 1) = \frac{1}{\lambda}, & r_t = 0 \\ 1 - H(r_{t-1} + 1), & r_t = r_{t-1} + 1 \end{cases} \]
The predictive distribution $P(x_t | r_{t-1}, x_{t}^{(r)})$ is a Student's $t$-distribution with updated degrees of freedom $\nu_t$, location $\mu_t$, and scale $\sigma_t^2$ under conjugate Normal-Gamma priors. To prevent catastrophic floating-point underflow over 120-month series, the recursion is evaluated strictly in log-space:
\[ \ln P(r_t=0, x_{1:t}) = \text{logsumexp}_{r_{t-1}} \left[ \ln P(r_{t-1}, x_{1:t-1}) + \ln H + \ln t_{\nu}(x_t; \mu, \sigma^2) \right] \]

\subsection{2. Terrain-Aware Distance Metric in 4D KDTree}
For work $i$ with latitude $\phi_i$, longitude $\lambda_i$, Terrain Ruggedness Index $\tau_i$, and District Capacity Index $\gamma_i$, the normalized contextual distance to candidate peer $j$ is:
\[ D(i, j) = \sqrt{ w_1 \left( \frac{\phi_i - \phi_j}{\sigma_\phi} \right)^2 + w_2 \left( \frac{\lambda_i - \lambda_j}{\sigma_\lambda} \right)^2 + w_3 \left( \frac{\tau_i - \tau_j}{\sigma_\tau} \right)^2 + w_4 \left( \frac{\gamma_i - \gamma_j}{\sigma_\gamma} \right)^2 } \]
Weights $w = [0.25, 0.25, 0.35, 0.15]$ prioritize terrain elevation and ruggedness over raw Euclidean proximity, ensuring mountain valley works are clustered with other mountain works rather than nearby plain headquarters.

\subsection{3. Bayesian Noisy-OR Evidence Aggregation}
When multiple layers trigger compound indicators, the calibrated probability of anomaly $P(\text{Risk})$ aggregates via the Noisy-OR formulation:
\[ P(\text{Risk}) = 1 - \prod_{k=1}^K \left( 1 - p_k \right) \]
where $p_k$ denotes the marginal risk contribution of layer $k$. For Layer 0 statutory violations, $p_{\text{statutory}} = 1.0$, guaranteeing $P(\text{Risk}) \equiv 1.0$ regardless of statistical or ML model outputs.
