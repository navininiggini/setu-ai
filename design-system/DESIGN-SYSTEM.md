# Official Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026) Design System
**Statutory Presentation Standard & Guidelines**

## 1. Compliance Mandate
- **6-Slide Strict Maximum**: The deck must not exceed 6 slides in total (Slide 1: Title, Slides 2-6: Content).
- **Mandatory Headings Preserved**: Official slide titles and internal section pointers must match the official SIH evaluation rubric.
- **Clean Government-Grade Aesthetics**: High-contrast, highly legible professional presentation designed for evaluation screens and PDF conversion.

## 2. Palette
- **SIH Deep Navy**: `#0F2B5C` (Header bar, major titles, footer banner)
- **Saffron Orange**: `#FF6B00` (Sub-headers, key highlight chips, bullet accents)
- **Neutral White / Slate**: `#FFFFFF` canvas, `#F8FAFC` cards, `#CBD5E1` borders
- **Body Ink**: `#0F172A` (95% Charcoal Black for sharp readability)

## 3. Standard Header & Footer
- **Header**:
  - Left: Oval badge with Team Name ("Innovexa") and Team ID ("P84").
  - Center: Slide Title in bold navy (`PROPOSED SOLUTION`, `TECHNICAL APPROACH`, etc.).
  - Right: Official SIH 2026 header logo strip (`asset_2_Picture_1.png`).
- **Footer**:
  - Full-width banner in `#0F2B5C` with `@SIH Idea submission- Template` and Slide Number.

## 4. Visual Communication Standard
- Replace walls of text with **structured bullet points**, **implementation flowcharts**, **quadrant cards**, and **comparative benefit tables**.
